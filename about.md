## Education
As a foreign student, I began my adventure in France with a DUT (University Technical Diploma) in Biological Engineering - Biochemical and Biological Analysis at the IUT (University Institute of Technology) in Dijon. After my DUT, I continued my studies by enrolling in a Bachelor's degree in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology. I then continued my studies with a Master's degree in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology. After my Master's degree, I wanted to focus on working at the interface between biotechnology and innovation. So, I decided to continue my studies with a Master's degree in Management and Innovation in Biotechnology. I would like to combine cutting-edge technologies in biology with real-world problems in order to find innovative solutions.
