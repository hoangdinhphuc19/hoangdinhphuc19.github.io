<h3>Role of bacterial functional redundancy in soil resistance and resilience to stresses : Functional inference from metabarcoding (RESOCO)</h3>
- Grants : **Grand Est region** and **LUE EPHemeris**
- Role : PhD candidate, Investigator
- Missions : 
+ Aplication of divers stress such as hydrate stress, metalic pollution, chemical physic stress on meadow soil from Grand Est
+ Monitoring evolution of functional capacity of soil microbial communities by a functional proxy like microbial respiration
+ Quantify soil organic matter components (C/N, macro-biomolecules such as protein, carbohydrate and lipid content,...) in soil through the stress
+ Analyse taxonomic and functional structure of microbial communities by molecular approachs like qPCR and high-throughtput sequencing (amplicon 16S)
+ Bioinformatics : pipeline FROGS for assesing the taxonomic composition + PICRUSt2 for functional inference + BactoTraits for prediction of bacterial traits
+ Data Science & Modeling: Utilized mixOmics to integrate functional inference data with in-situ functional measurements. Employed Random Forest models to evaluate variable importance.
+ Ecosystem Metrics: Developed novel soil health metrics focusing on functional resistance, resilience, and recovery under stress.
